How to play

  1. Each puzzle is a chain of words. The first and last words are given.
  2. Every neighboring pair forms a compound word or phrase — like hot dog, dog house.
  3. Tap a blank word, then guess letters on the keyboard, or type the whole word.
  4. Stuck? Tap Hint to reveal one letter at a time.
  5. A clean solve is worth 100 points. Each revealed letter costs 8 points (min 10).
  6. Solve every link to finish the chain. Fewer hints, more stars.
